Forge Oracle

Every roll at the Forge of Potential is fixed by your character's name. Type it in to see which weapons and armour will take your idols furthest.

Age

The number is the level each item reaches from level 3 before anything breaks. Open an item to aim higher.

How this works

Every Forge of Potential attempt is decided in advance. Each item and level step has its own roll, and the first roll comes from your character name. An attempt, whether it works or not, moves that step on to its next roll. Reloading doesn't change anything.

A roll of 0.65 or less succeeds. A failure destroys the item and gives back 35% of its materials. These values come from the game data and are the same for every idol.

On a first run almost nothing passes level 7. The game's random numbers for neighbouring levels are related, so five successes in a row is very rare. Higher levels always cost some broken items.

The lists assume this character hasn't used idols on these items yet, and that you bring each new copy of an item to level 3 yourself. If an item breaks, craft a new one, bring it to level 3 and follow the next run. Rolls belong to the item type, not the single item, so a new copy carries on where the old one stopped.
